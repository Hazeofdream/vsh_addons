    Balance Changes

    N = mercs at round start  ·  n = mercs alive  ·  H = Hale's max health

    General  (affects all classes)

    Control Point

    • Capturing the point no longer ends the round instantly. The point then locks for the rest of the round, and the stalemate timer is turned off.
    • Mercs capture: permanent crits on all weapons, plus a powerful 5 second health regen. Hale's health then drains faster and faster until he dies.
    • Hale captures: his abilities recharge faster, and his health regenerates faster and faster. If he reaches full health, he wins.
    • The drain or regen starts at 1 HP per second and goes up by 1 each second. If the side holding the point goes 30 seconds without taking damage from the other team, it speeds up by a further 1.05x every second until they're hit again.

    Round Timer

    • Setup lasts max(16, N/3) seconds, so big servers get extra time to spread out.
    • The point unlocks after max(30, 10N) seconds. During the round that's lowered to max(60, 15n) seconds as mercs die.
    • The round ends in a stalemate 3 minutes after the point unlocks, unless someone captures it.

    Glows

    • Mercs glow when only one is left alive, or after Hale captures the point.
    • Hale glows when only one merc is left alive, or after the mercs capture the point.

    Damage & Killstreaks

    • When you die, the damage you did to Hale is posted in chat.
    • At round end, chat shows the top 3 players, total damage, and damage from other sources.
    • Your killstreak goes up by 1 for every 200 damage you do to Hale, which powers Professional Killstreak effects.
    • Chat announces every backstab and market garden on Hale.

    Debuffs

    • Hale's debuff resistance is removed. Jarate and Marked for Death last their full duration on him (VSH normally halves them).

    Anti-AFK

    • Players who send no input for 60 seconds are warned, then killed. Hale's health is lowered to make up for them, as if they had never joined.

    Saxton Punch

    • Charges from damage Hale takes, not a 30 second timer.
      • One punch for every 50% of max health lost, while max health is 10,000 or less.
      • One punch for every 30% of max health lost, while max health is above 10,000.
    • Punches can be saved up and thrown one after another. Healing doesn't take progress away.
    • After Hale captures the point, he also gets a free punch every 60 seconds.

    Abilities

    • Brave Jump: 5 second cooldown, shown on the HUD.
    • Mighty Slam: no longer hits through walls; it needs a clear line of sight to each target. Weak slam hits (30 damage or less) can't kill players on 30 health or less.
    • After capturing the point: Mighty Slam recharges every 12 seconds and Sweeping Charge every 6 seconds.
    • Stun breakout: Hale breaks out of a stun after 2 seconds (was 4), or after losing 3000 health while stunned (was 1000).
    • Sandman lock: Scout's Sandman balls lock all of Hale's abilities for up to 8 seconds, depending on how far the ball flew. Saved punches are kept.

    Other

    • Losing Rock-Paper-Scissors does 1,000,000 damage to Hale (VSH's 100,000 isn't enough at high player counts), with very strong knockback.
    • Backstabs and market gardens do at most 5,000 damage. This only matters at very high player counts.
    Class
    Scout
    • Sandman: a ball that hits Hale locks his abilities (Mighty Slam, Sweeping Charge, Saxton Punch and Brave Jump). The farther the ball flew, the longer the lock, up to 8 seconds for a moon shot. Point-blank hits don't lock anything. Chat announces every moon shot.
    Soldier
    • Market Gardener: damage capped at 5,000. Chat announces every garden on Hale.
    Pyro

    No changes from standard VSH.

    Demoman
    • Shields: a shield broken by Hale comes back after you collect 6 more heads.
    Heavy
    • Miniguns: VSH's 30% damage penalty while crit-boosted is removed.
    • Natascha: slow on hit restored to its normal TF2 strength.
    Engineer
    • Sentry Gun: deals 80% damage to Hale (was 50%).
    • Mini-Sentry: deals full damage to Hale (was 80%).
    Medic
    • Crusader's Crossbow: every shot is a crit. Each bolt that hits Hale adds 15% ÜberCharge (it won't extend an Über that's already running).
    • Medi Gun & Kritzkrieg: work the same way apart from the Über type. Both build Über at the same rate and always last the full 8 seconds, even if you switch heal targets.
    • Ubersaw: adds 50% ÜberCharge per hit (was 25%).
    Sniper
    • Headshots: deal 1.8x damage to Hale (was 1.2x).
    • Sniper Rifles: each hit makes Hale glow for up to 8 seconds; 500 damage gives the full 8. Hits stack up to that cap.
    • Razorback: fully blocks one hit from Hale. Pick up a medium or large ammo pack to recharge it.
    Spy
    • Backstabs: damage capped at 5,000. Chat announces every backstab on Hale.
